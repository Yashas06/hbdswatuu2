The First Time We Met

Six years of knowing you through a screen, and suddenly, there you were standing right in front of me.

I was honestly so nervous to talk to you. Even looking at your face felt different in real life — I was shy, awkward, and probably didn't know where to look or what to say.

But somehow, those nerves slowly disappeared. And what was supposed to be just a first meeting turned into almost five hours together.

Five hours of talking, laughing, and simply being together — after all those years of only knowing each other through a screen.

July 14, 2026

The Second Meet

Three days later, we met again — and this time, it already felt a little easier to be around each other.

We talked even more and spent time giggling about all those old memories — the way we used to text each other, those old screenshots, and all the little things that once happened behind a screen.

And somehow, even something as simple as having tea together in the morning became a memory I know I won't forget.

It wasn't just about what we did. It was about finally getting to live all those little moments together instead of only talking about them.

A Letter For You

Oye Swatuu,

Happy Birthday, Swatuu! 🎂❤️

I don't think I've ever really known how to explain what it feels like being around you.

There's something about your presence that makes everything feel a little lighter. I can be having a completely normal day, and somehow, spending time with you makes it feel like a day worth remembering. I don't always need us to be doing something special. Sometimes just having you beside me is enough.

I love how comfortable I feel around you, even though there are still moments when I get nervous, overthink things, or don't know what to say. Somehow, even those awkward moments feel special because they're with you.

Being around you makes me forget about time. A few minutes somehow become hours, and when it's finally time to leave, there's always this little part of me that wishes we had just a little more time.

I don't know exactly when you became this important to me. It wasn't one particular moment. It was probably all the little moments slowly adding up until one day I realised that having you around had become something I genuinely looked forward to.

You have this way of making me feel happy without even trying. And maybe that's what I appreciate the most — I don't have to pretend to be anything around you. I can just be myself, be stupid, laugh too much, say random things, and somehow it all feels right.

If there's one thing I hope you always know, it's that your presence means more to me than I probably say out loud.

I'm really grateful for every moment I get to spend with you, for every laugh, every conversation, every silence, and even the moments where we do absolutely nothing.

Some people become important to you slowly, without you even noticing.

You became that person for me.

And honestly, I'm just really glad you're here. ❤️

And there's one more thing I want you to know. I wished you Happy Birthday every single day this whole month. And somehow, today is the last day of me wishing you. A little part of me doesn't want this month of wishing you to end, because it gave me an excuse every day to remind you how special you are to me.

So this is my last “Happy Birthday” for this month, but definitely not the last time I'll remind you how much you mean to me. ❤️

Happy Birthday once again, Swatuu. ❤️
